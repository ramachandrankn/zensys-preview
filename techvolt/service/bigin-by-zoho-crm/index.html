Bigin by Zoho CRM, set up for your business

A simple CRM for small businesses. We configure it around how your team sells, move your data in, and train your people.

Bigin by Zoho CRM is Zoho’s pipeline-based CRM for small businesses. It keeps leads, deals, calls, emails and WhatsApp chats in one place and automates follow-ups. Zensys is an authorized Zoho partner in Chennai: we customise Bigin to your process, migrate data from spreadsheets or other CRMs, train your team and support you after go-live. You can also buy Bigin licences through us.

What Bigin does

  • Pipeline management

    Visual pipelines for each team or product, with connected pipelines that follow a customer from enquiry to delivery.

  • Contacts and customers

    Every lead, contact and company with its full history. Import spreadsheets and capture enquiries with web forms.

  • Multichannel communication

    Built-in telephony, WhatsApp, email and mass email, plus meetings through Zoho Meeting, Microsoft Teams or Zoom.

  • Automation

    Workflow rules for reminders, tasks and emails, plus Zoho Flow and Zapier integrations.

  • Team collaboration

    Activities, calendar view, @mentions, team pipelines and Microsoft Teams integration.

  • Data administration

    Roles and profiles, audit logs, stage transition rules and duplicate management.

  • Dashboards

    Track pipeline value and team performance, with Zoho Analytics for deeper reports.

  • Mobile apps

    Access customers and deals on Android and iOS while you are on the move.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bigin by Zoho CRM?

Bigin is a simple, pipeline-centric CRM from Zoho built for small businesses. It manages leads, deals, follow-ups and customer communication in one place without the complexity of a full enterprise CRM.

Is Zensys an authorized Zoho partner?

Yes. Zensys is an authorized Zoho partner in Chennai. We implement, customise and support Bigin, and you can purchase Zoho licences through us.

Can you move our data from Excel or another CRM?

Yes. We clean, map and import your spreadsheet or existing CRM data, merging duplicates so you start with a reliable customer database.

Does Bigin work with WhatsApp?

Yes. Bigin includes WhatsApp integration, so conversations stay attached to the customer record.

What does the implementation include?

Requirement discussion, a live demo, setup and customisation of modules, fields and pipelines, data migration, user training, go-live and ongoing support.
