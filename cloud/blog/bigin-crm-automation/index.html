Bigin CRM Automation: How Small Teams Save Hours Every Week

CRMBy Zensys Team6 August 20264 min read

Less manual follow-up. Fewer missed tasks. Here’s how Bigin by Zoho CRM automation gives small sales teams their time back.

Introduction

Small sales teams rarely have the luxury of a dedicated operations person chasing follow-ups, updating deal stages, or reminding reps to call a lead back. Most of that falls on the salesperson, in between actual selling.

This is where a lot of hours quietly disappear every week – not on selling, but on repetitive administrative tasks that a system could easily handle instead.

Bigin by Zoho CRM addresses this directly through built-in automation. As an authorised Bigin by Zoho CRM partner, Zensys helps small businesses set up these automations around how their sales process actually works, rather than leaving teams to figure it out on their own.

Why Manual Follow-Up Is Costing Small Teams Time

Without automation, sales reps end up doing double duty – selling and remembering. Every follow-up email, every task reminder, every deal-stage update depends on someone manually keeping track of it.

For a small team, this adds up fast. A few minutes lost per lead multiplies across dozens of active deals, and it’s usually the busiest reps who let follow-ups slip simply because there’s no system prompting them.

How Zensys Sets Up Bigin Automation for Small Teams

Rather than switching on generic automation templates, Zensys reviews a business’s actual sales process – how leads come in, how deals move through stages, and where reps typically lose time – before configuring Bigin’s automation tools.

This means the automation reflects how the team actually sells, rather than forcing the team to adapt to a rigid, one-size-fits-all setup.

Key Automation Features in Bigin by Zoho CRM

Workflow Rules

  • Automatically trigger actions when a record is created or updated
  • Send follow-up emails without a rep having to remember
  • Update field values automatically based on set conditions

Automated Task Reminders

  • Auto-assign tasks when a deal enters a new stage
  • Reminders ensure no lead is left unattended for long
  • Reduces reliance on personal memory or spreadsheets

Macros for Repetitive Actions

  • Run multiple actions with a single click
  • Useful for repetitive steps like sending a set of standard emails
  • Saves time on routine, repeatable parts of the sales process

Blueprint for Sales Process Consistency

  • Defines mandatory steps a deal must pass through
  • Keeps every rep following the same structured process
  • Reduces deals slipping through due to skipped steps

Email and Notification Automation

  • Automatic alerts when a high-priority deal is updated
  • Scheduled emails sent without manual intervention
  • Keeps the whole team informed without extra effort

Benefits of Automating Bigin CRM for Small Teams

More Time Spent Selling

Automating repetitive tasks frees up hours that reps can redirect toward actual conversations with prospects.

Fewer Missed Follow-Ups

Automated reminders and workflows mean leads don’t fall through the cracks simply because someone forgot.

Consistent Sales Process

Blueprint and workflow rules ensure every deal follows the same structured steps, regardless of who is handling it.

Lower Dependence on Manual Tracking

Teams rely less on spreadsheets or personal notes to remember what needs to happen next.

Easier Onboarding for New Reps

Automated processes mean new team members follow the same guided steps from day one, without needing to memorise the full sales process.

Teams That Benefit Most from Bigin Automation

Small Sales Teams Without Dedicated Ops Support

Automation fills the gap that a dedicated operations role would otherwise cover.

Businesses with High Lead Volume

Automated task assignment keeps a growing number of leads from overwhelming the team.

Real Estate and Field Sales Teams

Reps on the move benefit from automated reminders that keep follow-ups on track even when they’re away from a desk.

Growing Small and Medium Businesses

As deal volume increases, automation keeps the sales process manageable without adding headcount.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What kind of tasks can Bigin CRM automate?

Bigin can automate follow-up emails, task assignments, field updates, and notifications based on rules set for how deals move through the pipeline.

Is Bigin automation suitable for very small sales teams?

Yes. Automation is especially useful for small teams without a dedicated operations person to manage manual follow-ups.

What is a Blueprint in Bigin CRM?

A Blueprint defines the mandatory steps a deal must go through, keeping the sales process consistent across the team.

Can Zensys set up automation based on our existing sales process?

Yes. Zensys reviews how a business currently sells before configuring workflow rules, macros, and Blueprints to match.

Does automation replace the need for a sales team?

No. Automation handles repetitive administrative tasks, freeing the team to focus on selling rather than manual tracking.

With workflow rules, macros, Blueprints, and automated notifications, Bigin by Zoho CRM takes over the administrative work so teams can focus on what actually grows the business.
