Cloud payroll and HRMS software

Run payroll, attendance and HR from a browser or phone. No server to maintain, and statutory rules kept current for you.

Zensys Cloud Payroll is online payroll and HR software for Indian businesses. It processes salaries, records attendance through a mobile app or biometric devices, manages leave, appraisals, onboarding and full and final settlement, and keeps PF, ESI, PT and TDS calculations up to date. It is accessible from anywhere and suits multi-branch and growing teams.

What's included

  • Mobile attendance app

    Employees mark attendance from their phone in real time, useful for field staff and multiple locations.

  • Time and attendance

    Works with biometric or online attendance systems and feeds working hours straight into payroll.

  • Cloud payroll processing

    Salaries calculated automatically, in line with government statutory norms, with payslips for every employee.

  • Leave management

    Employees apply for leave and managers approve it online. Balances update and flow into payroll.

  • Performance appraisal

    A structured, transparent appraisal process kept alongside the employee’s record.

  • Onboarding

    Collect new-joiner details and documents and set up salary structures from day one.

  • Full and final settlement

    F&F calculated from attendance, leave and salary already in the system when someone leaves.

  • Statutory compliance

    Labour law and statutory requirements applied and reported, reducing compliance risk.

Who it's for

Cloud payroll suits growing companies, startups and businesses with branches, field staff or remote HR teams. It removes the need for an in-house server and gives managers access from wherever they are.

Frequently asked questions

What is cloud payroll software?

Payroll and HR software that runs online. Authorised users log in from a browser or phone to process salaries, track attendance and leave, and download reports, without installing software or maintaining a server.

Can employees mark attendance from their phone?

Yes. The mobile attendance app records attendance in real time and sends it straight to payroll. It is useful for field teams, branches and hybrid staff.

Does cloud payroll handle full and final settlement?

Yes. When an employee leaves, the system calculates the settlement using the attendance, leave and salary records already on file.

Is cloud payroll suitable for small businesses and startups?

Yes. There is no server to buy, setup is quick, and the system scales as you add employees and locations.

Is payroll data secure in the cloud?

Access is controlled by user roles, and data is protected with encryption, backups and regular updates.

Is there an on-premises version?

Yes. If you prefer to keep payroll on your own server, see on-premises payroll software.
