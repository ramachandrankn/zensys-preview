Bigin CRM Multichannel Communication in 2026

CRMBy Swetha10 August 2026Updated 22 August 20264 min read

A call here. A WhatsApp message there. An email in a separate inbox. Bigin brings every one of these conversations into the same place a deal actually lives.

Introduction

Customers don’t stick to one channel. Some prefer a quick WhatsApp message, others expect a call, and many still rely on email for anything formal. For a sales team, juggling all of this outside the CRM means constantly switching between apps, and inevitably, some conversations never make it back into the customer’s record at all.

When communication happens outside the CRM, deals lose context. A salesperson picking up a lead doesn’t know what was discussed on a call last week, or whether a WhatsApp message was already sent and never followed up on.

Bigin by Zoho CRM addresses this by bringing calling, WhatsApp, email, and virtual meetings directly into the platform, so every conversation stays attached to the deal or contact it belongs to.

What Is Multichannel Communication in Bigin CRM?

Multichannel communication in Bigin refers to the ability to call, message, and email customers directly from within the CRM, across multiple channels, rather than switching between separate apps disconnected from the customer’s record.

Every call, WhatsApp conversation, or email sent through these integrated channels is logged against the relevant contact or deal, keeping a complete communication history in one place instead of scattered across different tools.

How Does Multichannel Communication Work in Bigin?

Sales and support teams can call leads and customers directly from within Bigin using built-in telephony, with call details automatically recorded against the contact. WhatsApp conversations can be managed from inside the CRM as well, giving the team full context on a customer before responding.

Emails sent or received are automatically associated with the right contact, and mass email campaigns can be run directly from the platform with performance insights available afterward. For meetings, Bigin integrates with Zoho Meeting, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom, so virtual conversations can be scheduled and joined without leaving the CRM environment.

Key Multichannel Features in Bigin CRM

Built-in Telephony

  • Call leads and customers directly from within the CRM
  • Call details automatically logged against the contact record
  • Removes the need to switch to a separate calling app
  • Keeps call history attached to the deal or contact

WhatsApp Integration

  • Manage WhatsApp conversations from inside Bigin
  • Full customer context available while chatting
  • Streamlines ongoing contact management
  • Keeps WhatsApp history tied to the CRM record

Why Multichannel Communication Matters for Sales Teams

No Conversation Gets Lost

When calls, messages, and emails are all logged against the customer record, no team member has to guess what’s already been discussed.

Faster Response Times

Real-time notifications through Signals mean teams can respond to missed calls or new messages without delay.

Better Handovers Between Team Members

If a different team member picks up a deal, they can see the full communication history (calls, WhatsApp chats, and emails) without needing a separate briefing.

Consistent Customer Experience

Customers can reach out through whichever channel they prefer, while the business maintains one consistent view of the relationship.

Less App-Switching for Sales Teams

Handling calls, messages, and emails from within the CRM reduces the time spent switching between disconnected tools.

Reporting on Communication Effectiveness

Email campaign insights help businesses understand what’s working, rather than sending messages without visibility into results.

Who Benefits from Multichannel Communication in Bigin

Sales Teams Managing High Contact Volumes

Teams handling frequent calls and messages benefit from having every interaction logged automatically against the right record.

Businesses Using WhatsApp as a Primary Channel

Companies where customers primarily reach out via WhatsApp benefit from managing those conversations without leaving the CRM.

Teams Running Email Campaigns

Businesses sending regular email campaigns benefit from built-in insights on open and engagement performance.

Remote and Distributed Sales Teams

Virtual meeting integrations support teams and customers connecting from different locations without extra scheduling tools.

Growing Small Businesses

As customer volume grows, having every channel connected to one CRM record keeps communication organised without adding separate tools.

Conclusion

Customers reach out however is easiest for them, and a CRM should be able to keep up. By bringing calls, WhatsApp, email, and virtual meetings into one connected platform, Bigin by Zoho CRM helps businesses maintain a complete, organised view of every customer conversation, no matter which channel it started on.
