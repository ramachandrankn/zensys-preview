Common questions

What does Zensys do?

Zensys is a Chennai-based software company, founded in 2008, that implements payroll software (on-premises and cloud), bulk SMS platforms and Bigin by Zoho CRM for businesses across India. It is an authorized Zoho partner and has completed 200+ payroll installations.

Should I choose on-premises or cloud payroll software?

Choose on-premises if you want payroll data to stay on your own server, need deep integration with biometric devices on the factory floor, or have complex multi-shift rules. Choose cloud if you want to run payroll from anywhere without maintaining a server and prefer automatic updates. Zensys offers both and can help you decide after looking at your setup.

Does the payroll software handle PF, ESI, PT and TDS?

Yes. Both payroll products calculate Provident Fund, Employee State Insurance, Professional Tax and TDS, and produce the statutory forms and reports needed for filing and audits.

Can it connect to our biometric attendance machines?

Yes. The on-premises payroll includes a time office module that pulls punches from biometric devices, handles multiple shifts, overtime and leave, and feeds attendance straight into the pay run.

Is Zensys an authorized Zoho partner?

Yes. Zensys is an authorized Zoho partner in Chennai. We implement and customise Bigin by Zoho CRM and other Zoho products, and you can buy Zoho licences through us.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Chennai and serve clients across Tamil Nadu and the rest of India.
