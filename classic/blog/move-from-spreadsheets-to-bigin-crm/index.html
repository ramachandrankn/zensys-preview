Move from Spreadsheets to Bigin CRM with Ease in 2026

CRMBy Swetha5 August 2026Updated 20 August 20264 min read

Upgrade from spreadsheets to Bigin CRM and manage your customers with ease. Keep all your sales data organised, secure, and accessible in one place.

Introduction

Spreadsheets are often where customer tracking begins. They’re familiar, flexible, and require no setup. But as a business grows, the same spreadsheet that once worked well starts breaking down: multiple versions circulate over email, formulas get accidentally deleted, and no one is fully sure which file is current.

Beyond the confusion, spreadsheets simply weren’t built for tracking relationships. They can’t send a reminder when a follow-up is due, they don’t log calls or emails automatically, and they offer no way to see a customer’s full history at a glance.

Moving to Bigin by Zoho CRM solves these problems, but the migration itself is often what holds businesses back. With the right approach, moving from spreadsheets to a CRM can be done cleanly, without losing existing data or disrupting an already-busy sales team.

What Does Spreadsheet-to-CRM Migration Involve?

Spreadsheet-to-CRM migration is the process of transferring existing customer, contact, and deal information out of spreadsheet files and into a structured CRM system like Bigin, while preserving accuracy and making the data usable for automation and reporting.

This isn’t simply a file upload. It involves reviewing what data actually exists, cleaning up duplicates and inconsistent entries, mapping spreadsheet columns to the right CRM fields, and deciding how historical records should be organised going forward.

How Does the Migration Process Work?

Migration typically starts with an audit of existing spreadsheets to understand what data is being tracked, where inconsistencies exist, and which records are outdated or duplicated. Column headers are then mapped to the corresponding fields in Bigin, such as contact details, deal stage, or company information.

Once mapping is complete, data is imported in batches and reviewed for accuracy. Duplicate records are merged, incomplete entries are flagged, and the sales team is walked through the new structure so the CRM is used consistently from day one.

Key Steps in a Spreadsheet-to-Bigin CRM Migration

Data Audit and Cleanup

  • Reviewing all existing spreadsheets and files in use
  • Identifying duplicate or outdated customer records
  • Standardising inconsistent formats (names, phone numbers, dates)
  • Removing irrelevant or incomplete entries
  • Consolidating scattered files into a single source

Field Mapping and Batch Import

  • Matching spreadsheet columns to Bigin fields
  • Creating custom fields for data your business tracks
  • Importing records in batches and checking each batch
  • Merging duplicates found during import

Benefits of Moving from Spreadsheets to Bigin CRM

One Reliable Source of Truth

All customer and deal data lives in a single system, removing confusion over which file is current or accurate.

Reduced Manual Errors

Automated fields, dropdowns, and validation reduce the typing errors and inconsistencies common in manually maintained spreadsheets.

Built-In Follow-Up Tracking

Unlike static spreadsheets, Bigin can send reminders and flag overdue follow-ups automatically, so nothing depends on memory.

Stronger Data Security

Role-based access in Bigin limits who can view or edit sensitive customer information, unlike shared spreadsheet files with limited access control.

Better Team Visibility

Managers can see pipeline status and team activity without requesting updates or chasing individual spreadsheet owners.

Easier Scaling

As customer volume grows, a CRM structure scales far more reliably than spreadsheets, which tend to become harder to manage as row counts increase.

Who Should Consider Migrating from Spreadsheets?

Growing Small Businesses

Businesses that started with a simple spreadsheet but have outgrown its ability to track a larger customer base.

Teams Using Multiple Disconnected Files

Businesses juggling separate spreadsheets for leads, customers, and follow-ups that are difficult to keep in sync.

Businesses Facing Missed Follow-Ups

Teams that have lost track of enquiries or follow-ups due to manual tracking limitations.

Multi-Person Sales Teams

Businesses where more than one person updates customer records and version conflicts have become a recurring problem.

Businesses Preparing to Scale

Companies planning to grow their customer base or team size and wanting a system that can support that growth.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will existing spreadsheet data be lost during migration?

No. With proper field mapping and batch imports, existing data is preserved and organised within the CRM rather than lost.

How long does a spreadsheet-to-CRM migration usually take?

Timelines vary by data volume and complexity, but a structured migration with cleanup and mapping keeps disruption to a minimum.

Can multiple spreadsheets be combined into one CRM system?

Yes. Multiple files can be consolidated during the audit and cleanup phase before being imported into Bigin.

Does the sales team need technical skills to use Bigin after migration?

No. Bigin is designed with a simple interface, and short onboarding sessions are usually enough for teams to get started.

What happens to duplicate records found during migration?

Duplicate records are identified and merged during the import process to keep the CRM clean and accurate from the start.

Conclusion

Spreadsheets can only carry a growing business so far. Migrating to Bigin by Zoho CRM gives teams a single, reliable system for customer data, without losing the records they’ve already built up over time. With a structured migration approach, businesses can make the switch smoothly and start using automation, reminders, and reporting from day one in 2026.
