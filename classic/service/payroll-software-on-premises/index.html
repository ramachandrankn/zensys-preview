On-premises payroll software

Payroll, attendance and statutory compliance running on your own server, set up and supported by the Zensys team in Chennai.

Zensys On-Premises Payroll is payroll and time office software installed inside your own network. It calculates salaries, pulls attendance from biometric devices, tracks leave and shifts, and produces PF, ESI, PT and TDS reports. Zensys has deployed it in 200+ installations since 2008, with a strong track record in the automobile sector.

What's included

  • Payroll processing

    Monthly salary processing with deductions and overtime worked out automatically, and a payslip for every employee.

  • Time office with biometrics

    Attendance flows in from biometric devices. Shifts, late entries and overtime are worked out before the pay run, even in multi-shift setups.

  • Leave tracking

    Leave types, balances and approvals kept in the same system, so loss of pay is calculated without a separate register.

  • Employee self-service

    Employees view payslips, apply for leave and update personal details without calling HR.

  • Statutory compliance

    PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS calculated automatically, with statutory forms and reports kept up to date.

  • Integrations

    Supports integrations with other enterprise systems and applications when your setup needs it.

Who it's for

On-premises payroll suits organisations that want full control over where salary and employee data lives: manufacturing plants, automobile component makers, financial services firms and companies with an in-house IT team. It also works well where attendance comes from several biometric devices across multiple shifts.

How we implement it

  1. Requirement discussion to understand your salary structure, shifts and devices.
  2. Live demo of how the software fits your process.
  3. Setup and customisation on your server: salary heads, leave rules and approvals.
  4. Data migration of employee records from your current system.

Frequently asked questions

What is on-premises payroll software?

Payroll software that is installed and runs on your company's own server or network, instead of on a vendor's cloud. Your payroll and employee data stays inside your premises and under your IT policies.

Does Zensys payroll integrate with biometric attendance machines?

Yes. The time office module takes punches from biometric devices, applies shift rules, late marks and overtime, and passes attendance directly to the monthly payroll.

Which statutory deductions does it handle?

Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax (PT) and TDS, along with the statutory forms and reports needed for filing and audits.

Can it handle multiple shifts and contract labour?

Yes. Multi-shift attendance, overtime and leave are managed in the same system, which is why the software has a long track record in automobile and manufacturing plants.

How many companies use Zensys payroll?

Zensys has completed more than 200 payroll installations across industries since 2008.

Should we choose on-premises or cloud payroll?

Choose on-premises if you want payroll data on your own server, need deep biometric integration on the shop floor, or have strict internal data policies. Choose cloud payroll if you want access from anywhere without maintaining a server. Zensys offers both.
