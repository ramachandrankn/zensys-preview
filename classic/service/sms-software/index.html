Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between transactional and promotional SMS?

Transactional SMS is triggered by a customer action or system event, such as an OTP, order confirmation or payment alert. Promotional SMS is used for offers and marketing campaigns sent to a list of customers.

Can the SMS platform integrate with our CRM or ERP?

Yes. The platform connects with CRMs, ERPs and other business applications so messages are sent automatically when something happens in your system.

Do we get delivery reports?

Yes. The reporting dashboard shows delivered, pending and failed messages and campaign performance.

Is support available outside office hours?

Yes. Zensys provides 24/7 support for its SMS service.

How long has Zensys provided SMS services?

Zensys has delivered SMS services to corporates and individuals since 2007.
