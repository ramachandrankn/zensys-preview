Authorized Zoho partner in Chennai

One team for your CRM, payroll and SMS. We implement Zoho’s Bigin CRM and make it work with the rest of your business systems.

Zensys is an authorized Zoho partner based in Guindy, Chennai. We implement and customise Bigin by Zoho CRM, migrate your data, train your team and provide ongoing support. Because Zensys also provides payroll software and SMS services, businesses can work with one partner for sales, payroll and customer communication instead of several vendors.

What you get from a Zoho partner

  • CRM implementation expertise

    Bigin set up correctly from the start, with modules, fields and pipelines configured for your process.

  • Business-specific customisation

    Every setup is configured around your actual workflow, not a generic template.

  • Data migration

    Records moved from spreadsheets, legacy systems or another CRM without disrupting operations.

  • Training and onboarding

    Hands-on training so your team uses the system confidently from day one.

  • One partner, several systems

    CRM, payroll and SMS from one team, with a single point of contact for support.

  • Licences through Zensys

    Buy and renew Zoho licences through your partner.

How implementation works

The same process applies whichever solution, or combination of solutions, you choose:

  1. Requirement discussion about your current workflow and challenges.
  2. Live product demo showing exactly how the solution would work for you.
  3. Setup and customisation of modules, fields and workflows.
  4. Data migration from spreadsheets or other systems.
  5. User training so your team is confident from day one.
  6. Go live and start working from the new system.
  7. Ongoing support for questions and adjustments.
